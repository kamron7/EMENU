How to swap
-----------
Drop your own model at public/models/phone.glb (same filename). The engine
auto-loads it, centers it, and scales it to ~2 units tall — no code change needed.
If your model has a dedicated screen mesh named "screen", the menu texture maps
onto it automatically; otherwise the engine projects the menu onto a plane at the
model's front face.
